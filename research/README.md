# Research workspace

Machine-readable registries and curated metadata safe for Git. Do not place downloaded archival payloads here automatically.

Current source-specific registries include:

- `documentary_sources.yml` — practical access posture for TNA/Colonial Office/HCA, customs/port books, Privy Council, and Jamaica/Curaçao/Barbados local archives. Read with `docs/DOCUMENTARY_SOURCE_ACCESS.md` and `docs/PRE_INGESTION_NORMALIZATION_POLICY.md`.
- `environmental_sources.yml` — period marine observations, tides/currents, Copernicus/NOAA contextual sources, and environmental evidence classes. Read with `docs/OCEAN_WEATHER_SOURCE_SETUP.md`.
- `shipwreck_museum_sources.yml` — archaeological wreck-site and museum/repository enrichment. Read with `docs/SHIPWRECK_MUSEUM_SOURCE_SETUP.md` and `docs/SHIPWRECKS_AND_MUSEUMS.md`.
- `art_sources.yml` — visual/map/material-culture source discovery and rights notes.
- `french_sources.yml` — French-source research lane.
- `places.yml` — curated place research inputs/aliases.
- `sources.yml` — main source registry and project-wide priority map.

Registries are discovery/access/policy aids, not canonical historical corpora. A registry entry never proves a ship identity, event, wreck relation, object relation, or historical fact by itself; those still require source-backed assertions and reversible resolution.
