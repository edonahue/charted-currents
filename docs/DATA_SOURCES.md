# Data source research index

Machine-readable registry: `research/sources.yml`.

## Foundation candidates

### CrespoDynCoopNet DATA Collections
1648–1778. Agents, activities, routes, freight, ships, companies, goods, events, legal trade, smuggling, illicit trade, Spanish Indies Trade.

https://datos.gob.es/en/catalogo/ea0041268-crespodyncoopnet-data-collections

### PARES / Archivo General de Indias
Primary documents, maps/plans, registers, fleet records, colonial correspondence.

https://pares.cultura.gob.es/pares/en/inicio.html

### SlaveVoyages
Trans-Atlantic and Intra-American forced-migration voyage evidence.

https://www.slavevoyages.org/

### NOAA ICOADS
Surface marine observations from 1662 onward.

https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/international-comprehensive-ocean-atmosphere-data-set

## Cartography / primary imagery

### Library of Congress
Priority reference: Herman Moll, *A map of the West-Indies... also ye trade winds, and ye several tracts made by ye galeons and flota...*, ca. 1715.

https://www.loc.gov/item/gm71005442/

### Greater Caribbean Mapping, 1450–1850
Searchable catalog of thousands of historical maps; downloadable catalog dataset.

https://greatercaribbeanmaps.org/
https://greatercaribbeanmaps.org/overview/data-download/

### Rijksmuseum Data Services
Dutch maritime art, ships, ports, portraits, navigational objects.

https://data.rijksmuseum.nl/

## High-value research/access targets

Naval Office Shipping Lists, British Online Archives colonial shipping collections, Maritime Britain, Prize Papers, Dutch Atlantic Connections, New Spain Fleets, Prize Papers-derived labor-market data, MarineLives, Nationaal Archief Sailing Letters inventories, letters of marque, and prize-court collections.

## Balance rule

Do not choose sources only because they are easy to ingest. Seek British, Spanish, Dutch, French, forced-migration, environmental, material/archaeological, and archival-map perspectives. Avoid accidentally building “British records with a Caribbean basemap.”
