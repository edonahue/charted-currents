# Historical and geographic scope

## Chronology

Primary working period: **1650–1730**.

First vertical slice: approximately **1685–1720** where source overlap is promising. Dates outside the range may appear for necessary context or entity continuity.

## Geography

Core Greater Caribbean:
- Caribbean islands and sea;
- Bahamas;
- Gulf of Mexico;
- Caribbean coasts of Central America;
- northern South America and relevant Guianas;
- Florida and adjacent Atlantic approaches.

Connected external endpoints such as Boston/New England, Cádiz, Sevilla, London, Amsterdam, and African embarkation regions may appear when directly connected to represented voyages. The product should still feel Caribbean, not like a generic Atlantic-world map.

## Initial focus

Port Royal-centered, trending toward broader Greater Caribbean coverage.

Priority connected places include Port Royal/Jamaica, Curaçao, Nassau/New Providence, Havana, Veracruz, Cartagena de Indias, Portobelo, Campeche, Bay of Honduras, La Tortuga (Venezuela), and selected New England ports.

## Place naming

Use modern canonical names for navigation/search with historical names and spelling variants attached. Never silently alter quoted primary-source wording.
