# Analytical ideas

These are research directions, not promised findings.

## Near term
- port connectivity;
- ship mobility;
- route frequency;
- traffic composition;
- source coverage.

## Signature future analysis: predation enrichment

Question: was a route/category disproportionately represented among captures after accounting for its prevalence in ordinary traffic?

Concept: `capture share for X / ordinary exposed traffic share for X`.

Only calculate when numerator and denominator represent comparable populations/time/geography, source biases are documented, small denominators are controlled, and coverage is adequate.

## Future questions

- Do privateering patterns shift with wars?
- Can postwar transitions from privateering to piracy be observed?
- Did hurricane years alter documented port connectivity?
- Which commodities were disproportionately exposed to capture?
- Which ports acted as inter-imperial brokers?
- Which vessels/people crossed legal roles?
- Can period navigation + winds/currents support plausible reconstructed routes?

Do not calculate a seductive metric simply because fields can be joined.
