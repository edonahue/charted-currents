# Source rights and ingestion policy

This is project governance, not legal advice.

A source may be historically useful without being suitable for automated ingestion, redistribution, or image reuse. Track access, metadata rights, database rights, text/transcription rights, image/scan rights, derivative permissions, attribution, restrictions, and unresolved questions separately.

## Initial source posture

### CrespoDynCoopNet
Strong foundational candidate. Government/CSIC catalog describes a GIS-oriented Atlantic trade database (1648–1778) containing agents, ships, routes, freight, goods, legal trade, smuggling, illicit trade, and Indies Trade records. Catalog links Open Data Commons Database Contents License 1.0. Ingest locally, preserve IDs, and re-check redistribution implications before publishing derivatives.

### PARES / Spanish State Archives
Strong primary-source and asset candidate. PARES states descriptive information and images of public-domain State Archive documents accessible through PARES may be reproduced/used without prior permission with required source attribution. Item-level public-domain status still matters.

### SlaveVoyages
Strong structured source with field/content nuance. Preserve source-observed versus imputed/project-created distinctions and re-verify current legal guidance before ingestion/publication.

### NOAA ICOADS
Strong environmental observation source. NOAA provides marine observations from 1662 onward. Early coverage is sparse; only display observations where records exist.

### Historical hurricane chronologies
Context candidate. Store reconstruction methodology and distinguish affected-location reconstruction from exact storm-center tracks.

### EKF400v2
Later reconstruction layer. Treat as reconstructed/contextual, never as exact observed shipboard weather.

### Library of Congress Geography & Map Division
Strong visual/primary-source source. LOC states digitized Geography & Map Division content is free to use/reuse unless an item Rights Advisory says otherwise. Check each item and preserve credit/citation.

### Greater Caribbean Mapping, 1450–1850
Strong discovery/catalog source. Project exposes a downloadable weekly refreshed catalog CSV. Resolve image rights at holding institutions.

### Rijksmuseum
Strong open visual/metadata source. Verify object-level rights despite broad Open Data/public-domain policy.

### Naval Office Shipping Lists / British Online Archives
High-value research target. v0.1 should use only clearly reusable/open records and small legitimate curated examples. Longer term pursue original archive/microfilm transcription and/or request BOA reuse/API/research permission. Do not scrape a licensed presentation.

### Maritime Britain
Use for validation/research/linking unless specific reuse permission establishes broader derivative rights.

### Prize Papers
Rights differ among metadata, transcriptions, and images. Review component-level terms before ingestion/publication.

## Review discipline

Re-verify rights before first ingestion and first public deployment. Record the review date in `research/sources.yml`.
